# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Contributors should demonstrate empathy, respect differing opinions, accept constructive feedback,
and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, trolling, personal attacks, sexualized
attention, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct. Report
unacceptable behavior through GitHub's private reporting and moderation tools.
